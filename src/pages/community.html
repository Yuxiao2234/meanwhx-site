Culture, not an intervention

Art, friendship, jokes, and coffee do not need to justify themselves as productivity tools.

MeanwhX can support a gentler research culture without presenting itself as a mental-health service. The point is simpler: technical communities are made of people, and people make culture around the work they care about.

Early-career scholars may be a natural center of gravity, but participation should remain welcoming to artists, engineers, writers, makers, and friends who share the spirit of the project.
