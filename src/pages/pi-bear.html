BEAR ARCHIVE / INDEX

Some objects quietly inherit the time in which we found them.

A bear may receive the name of a scientist, a dessert, a city, a theorem being learned, or something that made an ordinary afternoon unexpectedly good.

MeanwhX treats that attachment as part of the object, not an embarrassment to design away.

Personal archive stories are never published by default. They appear only when their owners want to share them.
