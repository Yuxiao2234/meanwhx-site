Ideas acquire form.

A design practice for jewelry, small objects, diagrams, mathematical form, and selected experiments in embodied intelligence. The work is exploratory: studies are labeled as studies, prototypes as prototypes, and unreleased production details stay private.

Current directions

A cabinet of studies.

Study 001Jewelry & objectsConcept

Wearable diagrams

Rings, pendants, desk objects, and small decorative forms informed by mathematical structure, language, learning, and human–machine co-presence—without requiring the wearer to decode a technical joke first.

Study 002Scientific topographiesResearch study

Structures made tangible

Selected scientific or mathematical structures can become visual and physical studies: optimization paths, embeddings, curvature, representation geometry, or other well-defined sources. The source should be named accurately rather than calling every form a “loss landscape.”

Study 003Natural commandsPrototype

Language becoming action

A small experimental branch around NL2CLI, embodied interfaces, and the cultural question of how people tell increasingly capable tools what they mean. Demonstrations remain clearly separated from live or safety-critical systems.

02

How work is shared

Open to conversation. Selective about production detail.

Finished public studies can include photographs, renders, process notes, and intellectual context. Manufacturing-ready CAD, unreleased designs, supplier details, and sensitive collaboration material should not be placed in the public website repository.

This is a practical boundary, not a claim that a website notice creates every form of legal protection. Before public disclosure of commercially important designs, consider appropriate professional IP advice.
