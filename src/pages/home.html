Studio / MEANWH[AT]

Drawings, mathematical structures, materials, interfaces, and ordinary curiosities become things that can be seen, worn, held, or played with.

Some are finished works. Some are studies. Some remain proposals. We would rather show an unfinished question honestly than pretend every experiment is already a product.
