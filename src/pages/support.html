Support & collaborate

Help the room become real without making it heavy.

We are open to support that preserves the project’s human scale: contributors, practical advice, fabrication help, thoughtful partnerships, grants, and—when appropriate—funding for a clearly scoped piece of work.

Funding posture

The work comes first. Funding should support the work rather than define it.

There is no need to choose “startup” or “nonprofit” before the project has earned that decision. A particular program might later use grants or fiscal sponsorship; a clearly commercial product might require a separate entity. Those are future structural decisions, not current claims.

We do not list funders, investors, partners, or financial targets unless they are real and approved for publication.
