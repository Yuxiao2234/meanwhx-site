Rights & use

Open to learning. Careful with extraction.

This page states the project’s working intent in plain language. It is not a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific license, contract, trademark search, design registration, or legal advice.

01

Working principle

Publication is not permission to manufacture.

Unless a work carries an explicit license saying otherwise, its creator retains their rights. A public page may be linked to and discussed, but copying artwork, reproducing product designs, manufacturing objects, reselling work, using MeanwhX branding, or commercially adapting material requires permission from the relevant rights holder.

Contributor writing and artwork should remain the contributor’s work unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. Publication terms should be agreed before posting.

02

Do not publish here

Keep sensitive design material out of the public repository.

  • Manufacturing-ready CAD or source geometry for unreleased products.
  • Supplier details, pricing, private contracts, or collaborator contact data.
  • Unreleased patentable or registration-sensitive material without advice.
  • Private drafts that a contributor has not approved for publication.
  • Secrets, API keys, credentials, or personal information.

Before commercialization

Decide what to protect, what to publish, and what to license before the object travels farther than the conversation.

If a design may become commercially important, seek appropriate advice before detailed public disclosure. Copyright, trademarks, patents, and industrial or design rights protect different things and vary by jurisdiction.

This site intentionally avoids pretending that a homemade notice creates protections it may not create.
