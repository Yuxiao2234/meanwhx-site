The seminar is over.

The slides are closed. Somebody is still drawing on the board. Somebody else has forgotten whether the coffee is theirs.

No minutes are being taken.

This is usually when the conversation becomes interesting.

**After Seminar** is a recurring format for that margin: a question, a joke, a poem, a drink, a sketch, or three people continuing one thread because none of them quite wants to go home yet.

There is no requirement that the result become useful by morning.
