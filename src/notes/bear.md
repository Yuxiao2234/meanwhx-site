`∃ bear, s.t. everything is slightly better.`

This is not intended as a rigorous statement.

It is, however, empirically plausible.

A bear on a shelf can become attached to a theorem being learned, a city, a dessert, a difficult semester, a kind professor, a new job, or a period of life that did not have a name while it was happening.

The object need not explain any of this.

It only has to remain there long enough to remember.
