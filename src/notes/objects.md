Scientific images can be beautiful without becoming decoration.

The interesting step is not to print a technical picture onto an object and call the result interdisciplinary. It is to ask which properties survive translation.

A curve may become a boundary. A topology may become a void. A trajectory may become a thread. A relation may become the rule that determines how parts meet.

The source still matters.

If a form comes from an optimization landscape, say that. If it comes from an embedding, a dynamical system, a hand sketch, or nothing more formal than a good visual instinct, say that instead.

Precision does not make an object colder. It simply gives the story somewhere honest to begin.
