## Field instructions

- Pick a coordinate.
- Arrive somewhere else.
- If confused, find coffee.
- Do not optimize this process.

The navigator is deliberately approximate. A useful room does not always begin with a complete map.

The purpose of MeanwhX is not to turn every informal thought into a program. Some things improve when they remain small enough to be changed by the people actually sitting around the table.

> A wrong map can still lead to a good conversation.

That is enough for version zero.
